### Independence test: is congestion independent of clear weather?

Two events A and B are independent when P(A n B) = P(A) x P(B).

- Observed P(Congestion n Clear) = **0.036574**
- Expected under independence, P(Congestion) x P(Clear) = 0.147291 x 0.277799 = **0.040917**
- Absolute difference = **-0.004343**
- Observed / expected = **0.8938**
- Chi-square = 35.92, df = 1, p = 2.059e-09, phi = 0.0273

**Conclusion: the two events are NOT independent.** The observed joint probability sits 10.6% below the independence expectation, so congestion and clear weather co-occur less often than chance alone would produce. The chi-square test rejects independence (p = 2.06e-09), but the phi coefficient of 0.0273 shows the association is very weak: with roughly 48,000 observations even a trivial departure from independence is statistically significant. Statistical significance here is a statement about sample size, not about practical importance.
